Told by Things

Everyday inventions tell their own stories.

Every day, a new 30-second animated short on YouTube: a safety pin, a microwave or a sticky note tells you, in its own words, how it came to be. The stories are dramatized, but the facts are real and checked against reliable sources.

Your host is Leo, a young inventor in a red cap, who closes every episode with a question for you.
